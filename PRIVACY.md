# BusyRoad privacy notice

BusyRoad uses the coordinates of the city selected in its BUSY Bar settings
to fetch current weather. It uses San Francisco as its default city. Each weather
request sends the latitude and longitude, rounded to four decimal places, to
[Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/). Open-Meteo also receives the network
information needed to reply, such as the source IP address.

Location, temperature units, and the wiper interval are stored in the app
settings on BUSY Bar. The app also stores its last successful weather response
and the associated coordinates on the device. It can use this saved weather
for up to three hours and replaces the record after the next successful request.

Searching for a city is handled by the BUSY Bar settings interface. That
interface sends the search query to the BUSY city search service. See the
[BUSY privacy policy](https://busy.app/policies/privacy-policy).
